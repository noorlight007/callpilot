Frequently Asked Questions

Does CallPilot integrate directly with Recruit CRM?

Yes, the Recruit CRM integration is live. Applicant status and submitted documents sync straight into the applicant record automatically.

How does the traffic-light scoring work?

After each AI screening call, CallPilot automatically scores the applicant amber, green, or red against the job brief. Amber means qualified and documents requested, green means documents received, and red means unsuccessful. Recruiters never have to review the scoring themselves.

How much does AI applicant screening cost?

Plans start at $395/month for 100 screenings ($3.95 each). Growth is $1,400/month for 400 screenings, Pro is $2,950/month for 1,000 screenings, and Enterprise pricing is custom for 2,000+. New clients get their first 100 AI screening calls free.

What happens once an applicant is qualified?

Qualified applicants automatically receive a WhatsApp (or SMS) message asking for a photo ID and their qualifications. Once submitted, it's automatically saved to the Recruit CRM applicant record, the status automatically updates, and the recruiter gets an email to verify it.
