From Application to Screened in One Call

No queue, no backlog, no chasing paperwork. Here's what happens the moment someone applies to a job posted through JobAdder.

1

An applicant applies to your JobAdder ad, or a recruiter adds one to a job

CallPilot dials automatically, usually inside the hour, any time of day or night.

2

CallPilot runs the screening interview

It reads the job spec from the advert and asks the qualifying questions in a natural, human-like voice conversation.

3

The call is scored the second it ends

Traffic-light scoring runs automatically. Nobody has to listen back or double-check the result.

4

Qualified applicants are messaged instantly

A WhatsApp or SMS request for ID and qualifications, with a secure email upload link if they'd rather use that.

5

Documents land straight in JobAdder

Whatever they send back updates the applicant's status and attaches the files to their record automatically.

6

One email, ready to verify

The recruiter gets notified once and confirms. No spreadsheet, no follow-up calls, no lost paperwork.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the JobAdder integration actually live?

Yes. It's live now, applicant status and documents sync into the JobAdder record automatically as soon as CallPilot has them.

How does the traffic-light scoring work?

Every screening call is scored amber, green, or red against the job brief the moment it ends. Amber is qualified and awaiting documents, green is qualified and documents received, red is unsuccessful. No recruiter has to sit and mark it manually.

How much does AI applicant screening cost?

Plans start at $395/month for 100 screenings ($3.95 each). Growth is $1,400/month for 400 screenings, Pro is $2,950/month for 1,000 screenings, and Enterprise pricing is custom for 2,000+. New clients get their first 100 AI screening calls free.

What happens after an applicant qualifies?

They're messaged automatically over WhatsApp or SMS for a photo ID and their qualifications. Once sent, it's attached to their JobAdder record, the status flips over, and the recruiter gets one email to sign it off.
